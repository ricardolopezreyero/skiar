# Logistics and budget: family ski trip from Torreón to Colorado (or New Mexico / Utah), 2026-27 season

Research date: 10 October 2026. Family: 2 adult skiers and 1 beginner child (two age bands, 3-6 and 7-12). Trip of about 6 days and 5-6 nights. Target budget US$6,000, flexible to US$8,000.

**Limits of this research (read first).** WebFetch failed with DNS errors on every domain tried: vivaaerobus.com, vailresorts.com, travel.state.gov, denvergazette.com, snowthere.com, nmas.com.mx and endlesstravelplans.com. Partway through, the shared web-search budget also ran out. So most facts below come from search-result summaries of the cited pages, not from full-page reads.

Most resorts have not yet published 2026-27 ski school and day-ticket prices. Taos day-ticket prices are explicitly "TBA". Many price inputs are therefore 2025-26 figures or third-party estimates, and they are flagged as such. Every number in the budget model is labelled as sourced, derived or estimate.

## 1. Flights: MTY to DEN, SLC, ABQ and the regional ski airports; Torreón (TRC) options; baggage

### Takeaway
The only nonstop from Monterrey to the ski states is Viva Aerobus MTY-DEN, about 2h40. Sources conflict on how often it flies: twice a week (Thu/Sun) or daily. Observed round-trip fares are roughly US$200-525 per person. Torreón has a nonstop to Dallas (DFW) on American, and that is the one TRC option that could beat driving to Monterrey. It is especially useful for Steamboat, because DFW-HDN has nonstops this winter. No nonstops were found from MTY to SLC, ABQ, EGE, HDN or ASE.

### Inferences
- **Best default:** drive Torreón-MTY (the brief says about 3.5-4h), park at Superpark (about MXN 1,400 or US$77 for a week), then fly Viva nonstop to DEN. Door to door to Keystone or Breckenridge is about **10-12 hours** (3.5-4h drive, 2.5h airport buffer, 2h40 flight, US immigration at DEN, rental pickup, 1.5-2.5h drive). An altitude night in Denver breaks this into two easy days.
- **The schedule is a real constraint.** If Viva flies only Thu/Sun, a 5-6 night trip doesn't fit neatly. The options are Sun→Thu (4 nights) or Thu→Thu / Sun→Sun (7 nights). Fixing this means either 7 nights of lodging or one leg on United via IAH/DFW. **Check the live schedule before choosing dates.**
- **TRC→DFW→DEN or HDN on American** is the main way to avoid driving to Monterrey. It is attractive for **Steamboat**, because DFW-HDN lands about 25 minutes from the resort. No fares were found, but connecting fares from a small airport are usually higher than Viva's MTY nonstop; that is an inference to verify. US immigration would then happen at DFW.
- **Salt Lake City (Park City/Deer Valley), Albuquerque (Taos), Eagle (Vail/Beaver Creek) and Aspen** would all need a connection via IAH, DFW or DEN, or via Mexico City. Fares were not found.
- **Gear:** rent it. Fees for ski bags plus boots on Viva/United (×3 people) and the hassle of handling them in a rental car would usually cancel out the savings of bringing your own, unless the adults already own boots. Carrying your own **boots** in a carry-on is the usual compromise.

## 2. Entry requirements: B1/B2 visas (including the child), I-94, passports

### Takeaway
Every family member, the child included, needs a valid Mexican passport and their own B1/B2 visa (an ESTA does not apply to Mexicans). Arriving by air, the I-94 is created automatically. Driving past the border zone requires a land-border I-94, now **US$30 per person**. If anyone lacks a visa, the roughly 4.5-month wait for a Monterrey appointment (May 2026 data) makes Jan-Feb 2027 tight.

### Cited Findings
- The B1/B2 visa fee is **US$185** in 2026 and is non-refundable even if the visa is denied. — [N+](https://www.nmas.com.mx/nacional/cuanto-cuesta-la-visa-americana-2026-tarifa-y-requisitos-mexicanos/); [Posta](https://www.posta.com.mx/nuevo-leon/cuanto-cuesta-sacar-la-visa-americana-en-nuevo-leon-tarifas-vigentes-y-pasos-del-tramite/vl2153538)
- A **US$250 "Visa Integrity Fee"** would be added on top, charged only if the visa is issued (total about US$435). Its status is **contested**: one source said early in 2026 that it wasn't being collected yet; another says collection would start in **October 2026**. — [Posta](https://www.posta.com.mx/nuevo-leon/cuanto-cuesta-sacar-la-visa-americana-en-nuevo-leon-tarifas-vigentes-y-pasos-del-tramite/vl2153538); [Tiempo.com.mx](https://www.tiempo.com.mx/noticia/visa-octubre-precio-alto-10000/); [El Financiero](https://www.elfinanciero.com.mx/border/2025/09/24/trump-cambia-costos-de-la-visa-de-eu-para-mexico-e-impone-nueva-tarifa-esto-debes-saber/)
- Monterrey's average wait for a visa appointment was about **4.5 months** (State Department data of 18 May 2026), with first appointments showing from October 2026. — [Infobae](https://www.infobae.com/mexico/2026/05/28/visa-americana-2026-las-fechas-disponibles-para-tramitarla-en-mexico-y-cuanto-tiempo-hay-que-esperar-segun-la-ciudad/)
- The **land-border I-94 fee rose to US$30** (a $6 base plus a $24 surcharge under HR-1) on **30 September 2025**, and the surcharge is indexed to inflation. **Air arrivals get an automatic I-94 and don't pay this fee.** — [Berardi Immigration Law](https://berardiimmigrationlaw.com/new-cbp-immigration-fees-in-effect-september-30-2025-what-travelers-need-to-know/); [El Imparcial](https://www.elimparcial.com/mundo/2025/09/22/que-es-el-permiso-i-94-que-estados-unidos-subira-un-400-de-6-a-30-dolares-como-afectara-a-los-mexicanos/)
- Land arrivals need an I-94 to go **more than 25 miles** inside the US (one source says 30 km, probably a conversion error) or to stay more than 30 days. — [El Imparcial](https://www.elimparcial.com/mundo/2025/09/22/que-es-el-permiso-i-94-que-estados-unidos-subira-un-400-de-6-a-30-dolares-como-afectara-a-los-mexicanos/); [Vanguardia](https://vanguardia.com.mx/noticias/mexico/suben-a-30-dolares-permiso-de-viaje-utilizado-por-turistas-en-eu-NA17535769); [Chic Magazine](https://www.chicmagazine.com.mx/consejos/requisitos-cruzar-frontera-estados-unidos-2026)
- The I-94 can be requested **up to 7 days before** crossing, through the I-94 website or the **CBP One** app. The fee is non-refundable. — [ViveUSA](https://www.viveusa.mx/educacion/cuanto-cuesta-el-permiso-i-94-en-2026-guia-rapida-para-mexicanos-que-cruzan-por-tierra-a-estados-unidos/)

### Inferences
- **Driving route:** apply for 3 provisional I-94s in CBP One (3 × $30 = **$90**) up to 7 days before. Then finish the process at the El Paso port of entry. Colorado and Taos are far outside the border zone.
- **Flying route:** the I-94 is automatic at DEN (or wherever the family first lands in the US), so there's no land fee.
- **Visa check (do this first):** check all 3 visas are valid through the return date. **The child's visa is the one most likely to have expired**, because visas for minors are often issued with a shorter validity. If anyone needs a new visa, budget $185, or $435 if the integrity fee applies, per person. Book an appointment now and check other consulates (Nuevo Laredo, Ciudad Juárez, Hermosillo, etc.), because the Monterrey wait (May data) runs into early 2027.
- **Passports** must be valid for the whole trip. A child traveling with both parents needs no special permit to leave Mexico (this is general knowledge, not verified this session).

### Gaps
- Whether children under 14 still qualify for interview waivers (rules narrowed in 2025) could not be verified; the search budget ran out. Check on the official visa portal (ais.usvisa-info.com / mx.usembassy.gov).
- Whether the Visa Integrity Fee is being collected as of October 2026 is not confirmed.
- No official CBP page could be read (WebFetch failed).

## 3. Driving: Torreón → Chihuahua → Juárez/El Paso → New Mexico → Colorado (distances, times, overnights, safety, tolls, border, insurance, Traction Law, fuel)

### Takeaway
Driving is cheap but slow and the riskiest option. Torreón-Ciudad Juárez is about **740 km**, plus a border crossing of **about 1-1.5h** in general lanes. El Paso-Breckenridge is **about 624 miles, around 11h**. Colorado is effectively **2.5-3 days each way**, and Taos is about **2 days each way**. That leaves at most **1-2 ski days in a 6-day window**. The State Department rates **Chihuahua Level 3 ("Reconsider Travel")**. Driving only makes sense for **Taos, with an 8-9 day trip**, and only in daylight.

### Inferences
- **Realistic driving itineraries (my estimates):**
  - **Taos:** Day 1 Torreón→Juárez (about 740 km, roughly 8-9h of driving with stops), then the border (about 1-1.5h), sleeping in El Paso. Day 2 El Paso→Taos Ski Valley (my estimate is about 410-420 miles and roughly 6.5-7h: El Paso-Albuquerque about 265 mi on I-25, which is unverified, plus the sourced ABQ-Taos leg). That makes it **2 days each way** and **8-9 days in total for 4-5 ski days**.
  - **Breckenridge or Summit County:** Day 1 to El Paso, Day 2 El Paso→Santa Fe/Alamosa (about 6-7h), Day 3 on to Breckenridge (about 4-5h). **2.5-3 days each way**, so not compatible with a 6-day trip.
  - **Steamboat or Snowmass:** add another 2.5-4h beyond Breckenridge (estimate).
- **Safety rule if driving:** daylight only, toll highway (45D/49D), fuel only at large stations, no stops on the Jiménez-Chihuahua-Ahumada stretch at dusk, and track the trip with family. Leave Torreón around 6:00 so the border crossing is done before dark. Driving at night through Chihuahua with a child is the risk to avoid entirely.
- **Approximate costs for the driving option (estimates):** Mexican tolls are about MXN 1,000 each way, so **about US$110 round trip**. The sourced tolls add up to MXN 716 one way, and the remaining tolls are unconfirmed. Fuel for about 2,800 km round trip is **about US$300-350** for an SUV. The I-94s cost $90, the US liability extension about US$50-150, and 2 hotels on the road US$240-300.
- **Mexican-plated car in Colorado:** a car that isn't AWD needs M+S or mountain-snowflake tires, or chains, plus 3/16" tread. Most Mexican cars have all-season tires that may not qualify, so carry chains or AutoSocks.
- **Alternative: drive to El Paso and fly ELP→DEN.** It needs the overnight drive plus the border, and gains nothing over the Viva MTY-DEN nonstop. It is only worth it if MTY fares are very high or the Thu/Sun schedule doesn't work. It still requires the I-94 ($30 each). The ELP-DEN flights are unverified.

## 4. Ground transport in Colorado and altitude acclimatization for the child

### Takeaway
For 3 people, an **AWD SUV rental** from DEN usually costs the same as or less than shared shuttles. It also lets the family sleep in Denver the first night, which is the main altitude recommendation from Children's Hospital Colorado. **Epic Mountain Express** shared shuttles to Breckenridge cost **about $89 per person each way** ($72 with an Epic Pass), so about **$430-534 round trip for 3**. - **Recommendation for the report writer:** primary plan **MTY-DEN Viva → Keystone (or Breckenridge if the budget is $8k+)**, with a Denver night. Alternative with the best kid-friendliness and lowest altitude: **Steamboat** via DEN, or TRC-DFW-HDN. Budget or adventure alternative: **Taos by car**, only with 8-9 days and daylight driving.
