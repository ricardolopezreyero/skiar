## 1. Which complexes are true ski-in/ski-out, which are ski-in/walk-out, which need a shuttle, and which listing labels mislead?

### Takeaway
In Breckenridge, true ski-in/ski-out is concentrated at the bases of Peaks 7, 8 and 9: Crystal Peak Lodge, One Ski Hill Place, Ski Watch, Village at Breckenridge and Beaver Run. The Four O'Clock run complexes (Four O'Clock Lodge, Tyra, Pine Ridge, Riverbend) are mostly ski-in/walk-out to the Snowflake lift. Sundowner and Peak 8 Village are walk or shuttle properties despite some listing titles. In Keystone, only Lone Eagle and The Timbers in River Run are consistently called true ski-in/ski-out; Silver Mill, Red Hawk, Expedition Station and similar buildings are walk-to-gondola. Many listings call a unit "ski-in/ski-out" when it is a 3–10 minute walk in boots.

### Cited Findings

**Definitions and mislabeling (general)**
- One Breckenridge guide defines true ski-in/ski-out as clipping into skis at the building and reaching a lift with no shuttle or gondola. It says true ski-in/ski-out in Breckenridge is concentrated around Peaks 7, 8 and 9 at properties such as Crystal Peak Lodge, One Ski Hill Place and Beaver Run Resort, and it advises checking the distance to the nearest lift. It treats the term as "a spectrum" and separates true slopeside access from "ski-in/walk-out," where reaching the lift takes a short uphill walk or a ride — [The Peak Properties](https://www.thepeakproperties.co/post/breckenridge-ski-in-ski-out-condos)
- A Breckenridge property manager says many properties marketed as "ski-in/ski-out" are actually walk-to-lift, meaning a 3–10 minute walk in boots. It recommends confirming a listing's location relative to specific peaks and lifts rather than trusting the phrase — [Peak Property Management](https://peakpmgt.com/breckenridge-ski-resort-vacation-rentals-the-ultimate-guide/)
- Vacasa's Breckenridge ski-in/ski-out page says lift distance "varies from true slopeside access to short walks" and that each listing should specify it. Example: one unit described as less than 400 ft from the Quicksilver SuperChair at the base of Peak 9 — [Vacasa](https://www.vacasa.com/usa/Colorado/Breckenridge/explore/Ski-in-ski-out/)
- In one guest review a renter called a Breckenridge "Ski in Ski out" condo "misleading" and smaller than shown, and the host disputed it. Recent reviews are a check on both size and access — [Airbnb listing 9345854](https://www.airbnb.com/rooms/9345854)
- Group-size and occupancy limits matter, and extra guests may not fit on couches or air mattresses (relevant when 2–3 families share one place) — [AE Property Management](https://aepropertymanagement.com/blog/5-common-lodging-rental-mistakes/)

### Inferences
- **Classification for the report (researcher's synthesis):**
  - *True ski-in/ski-out (to the door and out to a lift):* Breck: Beaver Run, Village at Breckenridge, One Ski Hill Place, Crystal Peak Lodge, Ski Watch (Peak 8). Keystone: Lone Eagle, Timbers. Beaver Creek: Bachelor Gulch, Arrowhead, SnowCloud, Elkhorn Lodge, Townsend Place. Snowmass: Top of the Village, Timberline, Woodrun Place, Crestwood. Steamboat: Torian Plum, Ptarmigan House, Bear Claw (unit-dependent). Winter Park: Zephyr (110 ft). Copper: The Lodge at Copper, Copper One.
  - *Ski-in/walk-out:* Breck Four O'Clock corridor (Four O'Clock Lodge, Tyra, Pine Ridge, Riverbend, Sundowner). Steamboat: Kutuk.
  - *Walk-to-lift (often mislabeled):* Keystone Silver Mill, Red Hawk, Expedition Station, River Run Condos and Springs. Copper: Taylor's Crossing, Copper Springs. Winter Park: Fraser Crossing/Founders Pointe (150 yd). Vail: most Lionshead "75 yards from gondola" units.
  - *Shuttle:* Breck Peak 8 Village. Steamboat: Champagne Lodge, Trappeur's Crossing. Keystone townhome areas labeled "Shuttle To Slopes."
- The Four O'Clock run is the classic Breckenridge mislabel: you can ski home, but going out means walking to the Snowflake lift. The run's end depends on snow conditions, as Sundowner's own listing says.
- **For a beginner child, "true ski-in/ski-out" is less important than distance to the ski-school meeting point** (Peak 8 base, Peak 9/Village base or Beaver Run in Breckenridge; River Run in Keystone; base of the gondola in Winter Park). A walk-to-lift unit 100 yards from ski school can beat a "true" ski-in/ski-out unit higher on the hill whose ski home uses a blue run a beginner cannot ski yet.

## 2. Typical nightly rates (1BR / 2BR / 3–5BR), holiday contrast, cleaning and service fees, and local lodging taxes

### Takeaway
Observable snapshots (mostly fall 2026 or older) put Breckenridge and Keystone ski-in/ski-out condos at about **$250–$650/night for 1–3BR at value or mid-tier complexes**. Premium Peak 8/9 base properties and big homes run **$900–$3,000/night**: Village at Breckenridge "from $907," a Shock Hill home averaging about $1,520, and Peak 8 chalets about $1,500–$2,900 in January. Add the Breckenridge lodging tax of **12.275%**, which applies to cleaning fees too. Steamboat short-term rentals carry **~18.4–20.4%** in tax. Airbnb has moved to a host-only fee model, so guests see fewer separate service fees there; VRBO still often adds a guest service fee.

## 4. Short-term rental license caps and regulation affecting 2026-27 availability (Breckenridge, Summit County, Keystone, Steamboat, Vail)

### Takeaway
Breckenridge caps non-resort licenses by zone, and its residential and downtown zones are reported full with waitlists. The **Resort zone, where most ski-in/ski-out condo complexes sit, is uncapped**, so supply at slopeside complexes is not directly limited. Keystone (a town since 2024) and Copper (Summit County Resort Overlay) are uncapped. Steamboat's mountain-area resort condos sit in an uncapped zone but pay a 9% extra STR tax. No 2026-27 rule change that would reduce ski-in/ski-out rental supply for guests was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Breckenridge history:** in fall 2021 the town adopted a cap of **2,200 licenses** for non-exempt units, while about **1,469** condos with front desks and full-time security were exempt — [Colorado Sun (Sept 2021)](https://coloradosun.com/2021/09/21/colorado-resorts-breckenridge-short-term-rentals/); [Summit Daily](https://www.summitdaily.com/news/local/breckenridge-passes-short-term-rental-cap-on-1st-reading/)
- **2022 zone system** (effective **Sept 27, 2022**) replaced the blanket cap with zones: Zone 1 ≤ 1,620 units, Zone 2 ≤ 130, Zone 3 ≤ 390, and the Resort category became its own zone — [Summit Daily (regulations put into place)](https://www.summitdaily.com/news/breckenridge-short-term-rental-regulations-are-officially-put-into-place/). At the 2022 first reading, 79 units were on the waitlist — [Summit Daily (first reading)](https://www.summitdaily.com/news/breckenridge-passes-first-reading-of-short-term-rental-regulations/)
- **2026 status:** one guide says the downtown and residential zones are full and waitlisted as of July 2026 — [GoLocal VR 2026](https://golocalvr.com/blog/breckenridge-short-term-rental-regulations-2026-guide). Another (late 2025) lists Zone 1 at **1,680** licenses (which conflicts with the 1,620 in the 2022 ordinance) and says **the Resort Zone has no cap** — [SkyRun guide](https://skyrun.com/breckenridge/guide-to-short-term-rental-regulations-in-breckenridge-colorado/); [19pine](https://www.19pine.ai/short-term-rental-laws/colorado/breckenridge). An April 2026 guide lists Breckenridge Zones 2 and 3, Frisco, and all four Summit County Neighborhood basins as capped with active waitlists, and Keystone as uncapped — [Sold in Summit](https://www.soldinsummit.com/summit-county-short-term-rental-regulations/)
- **Breckenridge owner fee:** a regulatory fee of **$756 per bedroom per year** (an owner cost, but it feeds into rates) — [AvantStay](https://avantstay.com/str-management/breckenridge/rules); [SkyRun](https://skyrun.com/breckenridge/guide-to-short-term-rental-regulations-in-breckenridge-colorado/)
- A "Breckenridge Short-Term Rental Rules (September 2026)" guide exists but could not be read — [Summit Realtor](https://summitrealtor.com/breckenridge-short-term-rental-rules/)
- **Keystone:** incorporated **Feb 8, 2024**; took over STR licensing from Summit County on **Sept 30, 2024**. Reported to have no cap, no waitlist and no night limit, with an annual fee of $285 (another guide says $250) — [Sold in Summit (Keystone)](https://www.soldinsummit.com/keystone-CO-Real-Estate/keystone-short-term-rental-rules/)
- **Summit County (unincorporated, including Copper Mountain):** Resort Overlay Zone licenses have "no restrictions on the number of nights rented." Copper is uncapped with no waitlist. Neighborhood zones (rules approved Feb 2023) carry caps and a maximum of 35 bookings per year per property — [Summit County license page](https://www.summitcountyco.gov/services/community_development/short_term_rentals/license_applications.php); [Sold in Summit (unincorporated)](https://www.soldinsummit.com/summit-county-short-term-rental-regulations/unincorporated-summit-county-str-rules/)
- **Steamboat Springs:** Zone A ("green") has **no cap**. Zone B subzones are capped (for example 5 in B1, 20 in B2) — [Steamboat municipal code](https://library.municode.com/co/steamboat_springs/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIISTSPREMUCO_CH12LIPEMIBURE_ARTIXSHRMRELI_DIV1GE). Most mountain-area resort condos fall in the unrestricted zone — [Real Estate in Steamboat (2026)](https://realestateinsteamboat.com/blog/steamboat-springs-investment-property-short-term-rental-rules-zones-and-roi-2026/). A 9% STR tax applies on top (see section 2)

### Inferences
- For renters, the caps matter mainly for **single-family homes and in-town condos outside the Resort zone**, such as large Breckenridge homes in residential zones. Those listings may be fewer, pricier, or run by license holders with tight calendars. Ski-in/ski-out condo complexes at the Peak 7/8/9 bases are generally Resort-zone, so supply there should not be limited (inference: the Resort zone boundaries were not confirmed per complex).
- Because license fees ($756 per bedroom per year in Breckenridge) and STR taxes (Steamboat 9%) are passed to guests, large homes in those towns carry a structural premium.
- **Booking tip:** a legitimate Breckenridge, Keystone or Summit County listing should display a license number. Asking for it filters out unlicensed or scam listings (inference; no source specifically cited requiring display).

### Gaps
- **Vail** STR rules and 2026-27 changes were not found (search budget exhausted). The same goes for **Snowmass Village/Pitkin County**, **Winter Park/Grand County**, **Taos/Red River/Angel Fire** and **Park City/Summit County UT**.
- The current Breckenridge waitlist count and the exact current Zone 1 cap (1,620 vs 1,680) could not be confirmed on the town website, which was unreachable.

### Inferences
- **Booking-direct strategy (inference):** find the complex and unit on Airbnb or VRBO, identify the manager (unit numbers such as "Lone Eagle 3044" or "Pine Ridge 7E" are usually in the listing title), and price the same unit on the manager's site. This avoids VRBO guest fees and can unlock resort perks (Vail Resorts lodging: Kids Ski Free at Keystone, possibly lift-ticket or ski-school bundles). Under Airbnb's new host-only fee, Airbnb's price gap vs direct has narrowed for many listings.
- From Mexico, card foreign-transaction fees and deposit timing matter. Large deposits (up to 50%) due 60+ days out mean early February 2027 bookings need deposits by early December 2026.
- **Cancellation risk is high for an international family** (illness, flight changes, visa or document issues). A CFAR policy, typically about 50–75% reimbursement (industry norm, unverified here), or a manager with a 30-day cancellation is preferable. Booking by about November 2026 for a late-January or February 2027 week is sensible: inventory at the top complexes thins, and early booking keeps cancellation windows open longer.
- Avoid the BGV $139 offer unless the family wants to sit through a timeshare presentation. Grand Timber can also be rented normally (RedWeek and VacationRenter show standard rentals).

## 6. Rental vs hotel for this family (pros and cons)

### Takeaway
A ski-in/ski-out condo gives this family a kitchen, space and laundry, and lets several families sit side by side in one complex at a lower per-person cost. Hotels and hotel-condo hybrids such as One Ski Hill Place, Village at Breckenridge and Timbers/Lone Eagle by Vail Resorts add front desks, ski valet or lockers and resort perks. Pure private rentals lack daily housekeeping and on-site help, and carry stricter cancellation terms.

### Cited Findings
- **Daily housekeeping is not standard.** One Breckenridge manager sells daily maid service as an add-on at **$35/day** with 72 hours' notice — [Breckenridge Resort Managers](https://www.breckenridgeresortmanagers.com/policies); [Breckenridge Lodging](https://www.breckenridgelodging.com/rental-policies)
- **Check-in and out timing:** a typical rental is check-in 4:00 PM, checkout 10:00 AM — [VRBO 2982066](https://vrbo.com/2982066)
- **Ski valet exists at some condo-hotels** (One Ski Hill Place) — [Summit Mountain Rentals](https://summitrentals.com/vacation-rentals/one-ski-hill-place-condo-peak-8-ski-inout-resort-amenities). Private units often have a private ski locker instead — [Airbnb 43604542](https://www.airbnb.com.tw/rooms/43604542); [Big Horn Rentals](https://bighornrentals.com/booking/taylors-crossing-210/26324)
- **Parking fees:** Zephyr charges about $30/day in ski season — [VRBO Zephyr](https://www.vrbo.com/9642097ha). Fraser Crossing/Founders Pointe charge an extra daily fee for underground parking — [Play Winter Park](https://www.playwinterpark.com/winter-park/lodging/condos-townhomes-homes/fraser-crossing-founders-pointe)
- **Hidden resort fees:** Park City units can carry a mandatory ~$39.74/day resort fee not included in the booking — [Tripadvisor Canyons](https://www.tripadvisor.com/VacationRentalReview-g57130-d26811947-Chic_Ski_in_Ski_out_Studio_at_Canyons_Village-Snyderville_Utah.html)
- **Tax:** in Steamboat, hotels ("legacy lodging") are exempt from the 9% STR tax that rentals pay — [Simply Steamboat](https://www.simplysteamboat.com/New-Short-Term-Rental-Tax-Affordable-Housing.php)
- **Scams and misrepresentation risk:** see review disputes and mislabeled ski access in section 1 — [Airbnb 9345854](https://www.airbnb.com/rooms/9345854); [Peak Property Management](https://peakpmgt.com/breckenridge-ski-resort-vacation-rentals-the-ultimate-guide/)

### Inferences
- **Pros of a rental (inference):**
  - Cooking breakfasts and most dinners can plausibly save several hundred to $1,000+ over 6 days in Breckenridge or Vail, where restaurant prices are high.
  - A separate bedroom for the child (early bedtime) and a washer/dryer for ski clothes.
  - 2BR units often cost little more than 1BR units, leaving room for a guest family.
  - Same-complex multi-family stays (Beaver Run, River Run, Village at Breckenridge, Fraser Crossing/Founders Pointe) let each family keep its own budget and cancellation terms.
- **Cons (inference):**
  - No daily housekeeping, no concierge or ski valet (except condo-hotels), and self check-in or key pickup that may be off-site at the manager's office.
  - Late arrival after a Denver drive is harder.
  - Stricter cancellation terms and prepayment.
  - Cleaning fees, platform fees and 12–20% lodging taxes that make a low nightly price misleading.
  - Variable unit quality within the same complex (owner-furnished).
- **Recommended hybrid for this family (inference):** a **managed condo complex with a front desk** (Beaver Run, Village at Breckenridge, One Ski Hill Place, Timbers/Lone Eagle by Vail Resorts lodging, or Zephyr) gives rental space and a kitchen with hotel-like support. These are also the Breckenridge properties historically exempt from the license cap because they have front desks (section 4).

### Gaps
- No quantitative food-cost comparison (groceries vs restaurants) for ski towns was collected; this may be covered by the logistics/budget researcher.
- No hotel prices were gathered, by design (another researcher covers hotels).

## 7. Ranked shortlist per resort and overall top 10 (researcher's synthesis)

### Takeaway
For 2 adults and a beginner child on a ~$1,800–$4,000 lodging budget in a non-holiday late-January to early-March week, the best-value true ski-in/ski-out picks are Keystone **Lone Eagle and Timbers** and Breckenridge's Peak 9 base (**Beaver Run**, and **Grand Timber Lodge** as a value option). Breckenridge **One Ski Hill Place**, **Village at Breckenridge** and **Crystal Peak Lodge** are the premium and trendy picks. **Winter Park's base village** is the best value close to Denver. Shock Hill and Peak 8 homes are the multi-family splurge.

### Inferences
**All rankings in this section are the researcher's analytic judgment. Prices are estimates extrapolated from the snapshots above, not quotes.**

**Breckenridge (ranked for this family)**
1. **Beaver Run Resort condos (Peak 9)**: true ski-in/ski-out at the Beaver Run SuperChair, with the smaller Beaver Run ski school and childcare at the door. A huge complex, ideal for several families in separate units. *Budget fit: studio/1BR likely in range in non-holiday weeks (unverified).*
2. **Village at Breckenridge (Peak 9 base)**: ski-in/ski-out, Village ski school at the base, half a block to Main Street, pool. *Snapshot from $907, so likely over budget; check 1BR and studio rates.*
3. **One Ski Hill Place (Peak 8 base)**: the trendiest and most luxurious, with ski valet and the Peak 8 ski school and children's center at the base. *Splurge.*
4. **Crystal Peak Lodge (Peak 7 base)**: newer ski-in/ski-out; ski school at Peak 8 is one gondola stop or a ski down. *$284+ snapshot; good mid-tier option.*
5. **Grand Timber Lodge (BGV, Peak 9)**: value, near the Beaver Run school; avoid the timeshare-tour offer. *2BR from ~$250 (undated).*
6. **Four O'Clock run complexes** (Four O'Clock Lodge, Tyra, Pine Ridge, Riverbend): ski-in/walk-out, cheapest slopeside, walk to town. Weaker for a beginner's drop-off.
7. **Ski Watch (Peak 8)**: ski-in/ski-out at Peak 8; mid-tier.
8. **Shock Hill / Peak 8 homes (3–7BR)**: for 2–3 families at about $1,500–$2,900/night.
- *Avoid or verify carefully:* **Peak 8 Village** (shuttle). **Sundowner** (ski-in only, 5–7 min walk). Any "4 O'Clock run, short walk" listing that is not on the run itself.

**Keystone**
1. **Lone Eagle (River Run)**: true ski-in/ski-out, pool and hot tub; 2BR from ~$408 and 3BR from ~$535 (snapshot); Kids Ski Free if booked via Keystone Resort.
2. **The Timbers (River Run)**: true ski-in/ski-out, steps from the gondola; $258–$633.
3. **Silver Mill / Expedition Station / Red Hawk (River Run)**: walk-to-gondola value at about $180–$536; lots of units for multi-family groups.
4. **Ski Tip Townhomes (3BR ~$433)**: a quieter 3BR option for 2 families. Ski access not verified.
