## Q1. Which properties are genuinely ski-in/ski-out, and which are walk-to-lift or shuttle-dependent?

### Takeaway
In Breckenridge, the true ski-in/ski-out properties for families sit at the Peak 8 base (Grand Colorado on Peak 8, One Ski Hill Place), on Peak 7 (Grand Lodge on Peak 7, Crystal Peak Lodge) and at the Peak 9 / Beaver Run base (Beaver Run Resort).
- Mountain Thunder Lodge is "ski-in / gondola-out".
- BlueSky Breckenridge is beside the small Snowflake lift.
- Hotel Alpenrock's ski access is unclear.

Elsewhere, sources describe the following as true or near-true slopeside: Park Hyatt and The Osprey (Beaver Creek), Arrabelle and Lion Square Lodge (Vail Lionshead), Limelight and Viceroy (Snowmass), Torian Plum and One Steamboat Place (Steamboat), and Zephyr Mountain Lodge (Winter Park). Sources say these are NOT ski-in/ski-out: Manor Vail, Steamboat Grand, Mountain House condos at Keystone, and Trappeur's Crossing.

### Inferences
- **"Ski right up to it" in Breckenridge:**
  - The cleanest two-way ski-in/ski-out hotels are One Ski Hill Place and Grand Colorado on Peak 8, which share the Peak 8 base area, and Beaver Run at the base of the Beaver Run SuperChair (Peak 9).
  - Grand Lodge on Peak 7 is also true ski-in/ski-out, but it is in a quieter, more remote base area.
  - Mountain Thunder is "ski home, gondola up" (the ski-back trail plus a tunnel to the gondola), which suits adults but is less ideal for a beginner child.
- **BlueSky and Alpenrock:** treat them as "close to a lift, shuttle-assisted" until each is checked on a trail map.
- **Lift access is not the same as ski-school access:** most out-of-state luxury picks are ski-in/ski-out to the mountain, but not necessarily to the beginner ski-school area (see Q2).

## Q2. Which properties are closest to the children's ski school drop-off?

### Takeaway
Breckenridge has the clearest fit. The official children's lesson meeting points are inside or beside family lodging:
- the **Grand Colorado Children's Facility at Peak 8**,
- the **Village Children's Facility at the base of Peak 9** (in the Village at Breckenridge complex),
- the **Beaver Run Children's Facility**.

So for a beginner child, **Grand Colorado on Peak 8** (with One Ski Hill Place next door) and **Beaver Run Resort** are effectively "walk downstairs or next door to ski school." Elsewhere:
- **Snowmass:** the Limelight sits next to the Treehouse Kids' Adventure Center, and the Viceroy is about a 7-minute walk.
- **Beaver Creek:** a past guest says the Park Hyatt is "literally next door to ski school."
- **Vail:** Manor Vail is the walk-to option for the Golden Peak kids' school; the Arrabelle and Lion Square are at the Lionshead gondola.
- **Keystone:** the full-week Camp/Kamp Keystone program is at Mountain House, a shuttle ride from River Run.
- **Montage Deer Valley:** a family reviewer found the kids' ski school far away (two lifts).

### Inferences
- **Breckenridge order for kids' ski-school proximity:**
  1. Grand Colorado on Peak 8 (the children's facility is in the complex).
  2. One Ski Hill Place (adjacent at the Peak 8 base, near the top gondola terminal).
  3. Beaver Run (its own children's facility).
  4. Village at Breckenridge (Peak 9 Village facility; not otherwise researched).
  5. Grand Lodge / Crystal Peak on Peak 7 (they need the gondola or a ski run to Peak 8).
  6. BlueSky / Mountain Thunder / Alpenrock (shuttle or gondola needed).

### Takeaway
